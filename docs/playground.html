How a connection is answered

One port, one application, six ways in. Pick a client and follow it: the socket, the handshake or the preface, the first bytes that decide, and the loop that ends up serving it. Every trace is what the client actually received.

Where these come from. scripts/docs/record_traces.py ran each client against the HTTP/3 example (src/examples/07-http3) and the WebSocket echo example (src/examples/05-websocket-echo), and wrote down what came back. The dates are the dates of that recording. Nothing here is a mock: the application answers with http_version, which is why the body of each trace reads 1.1, 2 or 3.
